About this event
Join us on September 15, 2026, for the 2026 APL S&T Showcase — an event featuring pioneering research, emerging technologies, and innovative concepts from across APL. Through interactive demonstrations and posters, the Showcase will highlight cutting-edge research and development efforts advancing critical capabilities, exploring future mission needs, and addressing evolving national security challenges and priorities. Attendees will have the opportunity to engage directly with APL technical experts, experience cutting-edge work underway across the Laboratory, and explore the breadth of innovation shaping APL’s future mission impact.

There will be two sessions: a morning session from 8:30 a.m. to noon for government and contractor attendees, and an afternoon session from 1 to 4 p.m. for academia, industry and community attendees.

Technical Focus Areas:

AI, Autonomy, and Teaming
Sensing, C5ISR, and Networking
Hypersonics, EW, and Directed Energy
Cybersecurity and Mission Assurance
Advanced Manufacturing and Contested Logistics
Biotechnology and Medical Systems
Space Systems and PNT
Microelectronics, Quantum, and Materials
