# Feature Booths:
- Achieving Robust Homeland Defense
    - APL is advancing resilient critical infrastructure to counter kinetic, cyber, and other non-kinetic threats while enabling rapid response. Emerging technologies are key to building a more secure and resilient future.
- Revitalizing the Nation's Strategic Capabilities
    - The Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory is leveraging decades of experience with sea- and land-based strategic systems to lead development of flexible, adaptable, and integrated next-generation nuclear and conventional deterrent capabilities for the Navy and Air Force.
- Accelerating Warfighting Capabilities for the Pacific Theater
    - APL is committed to accelerating the development and fielding of new, compelling capabilities through responsive mission engineering and integrated prototyping. Prioritizing capabilities that sustain operational access and reinforce resilient logistics and supply chains in contested environments.
- Enabling Space Superiority
    - With space now a contested but essential domain for security, commerce, and daily life, the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory is leveraging six decades of experience to help achieve U.S. space superiority through advanced science, responsible space control, and innovative capabilities from Earth orbit to cislunar space.
